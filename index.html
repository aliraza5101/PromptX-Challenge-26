The Challenge

What is PROMPTX?

A first-of-its-kind mock virtual AI hackathon where creativity meets intelligence. No heavy coding required — just AI, prompting, and purpose.

🧠
GENERATIVE AI FIRST
Every challenge is built around real AI tools — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Runway, Canva AI, and more. AI is your entire toolkit.
🎯
SELECTION ROUND
This mock hackathon selects the top-performing teams to compete in the upcoming onsite, in-person hackathon. Perform well — move on.
💡
NO CODING REQUIRED
Smart prompting, creative thinking, and no-code platforms are all you need. Every challenge can be completed without writing a single line of code.
🏆
5 CHALLENGES
Five distinct domains — prompt engineering, visual AI, branding, AI system design, and product development — in one 8-hour sprint.
The Gauntlet

Five Challenges. Eight Hours.

Each challenge tests a different dimension of AI creativity. All five must be completed within the 8-hour hackathon window.

01
📐
The Prompt Architect
PROMPT ENGINEERING
Five broken prompts from real AIS departments. Diagnose what's wrong, rebuild them using proven techniques — role assignment, chain-of-thought, few-shot — and prove the improvement with live AI output.
⏱ 60 min ◆ Moderate
02
🎬
AI Storyboard Studio
VISUAL AI & CREATIVITY
Turn an AIS campaign brief into a 6–8 frame visual storyboard using AI image generators and writing tools. Think like a creative agency. Plan it, prompt it, build it, pitch it.
⏱ 60 min ◆ Moderate
03
🎨
Brand the AIS
BRANDING & CONTENT
Starting from the AIS logo, build a complete professional brand system — colour palette, typography, voice guide, visual rules — then produce a full one-week social media content calendar.
⏱ 75 min ◆ Moderate
04
🤖
Build ARIA
AI SYSTEM DESIGN
Design, build, and demo AIS's own intelligent AI assistant — ARIA. Write her personality, knowledge base, and system prompt. Deploy on a no-code platform and let judges interact with her live.
⏱ 75 min ◆◆ Moderate+
05
🚀
Build for AIS
AI PRODUCT — FLAGSHIP ★
150 minutes. A real AIS problem. Every AI tool available. Build a functional, demonstrable AI-powered product that makes some part of AIS's work faster, smarter, or better. It must work when a judge uses it.
⏱ 150 min ★ Hard

Guidelines

Rules & Regulations

👥
TEAM SIZE
Exactly 4 members per team. Every member must contribute. All five challenges must be attempted within the 8-hour window. No extensions under any circumstance.
⚡
AI TOOLS REQUIRED
This is a Generative AI hackathon. You must use AI tools — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Canva AI, Runway, and others. Document every tool used for every challenge or lose marks.
🚫
ZERO PLAGIARISM
All work must be created during the hackathon window. Pre-built content, copied outputs, or sharing between teams results in immediate disqualification. No exceptions.
📁
SUBMISSION FORMAT
All deliverables submitted in a structured Google Drive folder with exactly 5 sub-folders named as specified. Public link submitted via the official Google Form before the deadline.
🌐
ONLINE CONDUCT
Stay connected to the official WhatsApp/Discord channel at all times. Remain professional and respectful. All content must be written in clear English.
⚖️
FAIR JUDGING
All teams start simultaneously. Each challenge is scored independently. Total score across all 5 challenges determines the winner. Judges' decisions are final and non-appealable.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need coding skills to participate? ▼
No coding experience is required at all. Every challenge is designed to be completed using AI tools, smart prompting, no-code platforms, and creative thinking. Students with coding skills may use them as a bonus, but it's absolutely not required.
What AI tools are we allowed to use? ▼
You are encouraged to use any and all AI tools — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Canva AI, Adobe Firefly, DALL-E, Runway, CapCut AI, Poe, Perplexity, and more. For the flagship challenge (05), literally everything is permitted. Document which tools you use for every challenge.
When are the problem statements released? ▼
Full problem statements and scenario cards are released to all teams at exactly 12:00 PM on 25 May 2026, when the hackathon goes live. The reveal button on this website also activates at that time. Broken down challenge overviews are available above so you can prepare.
Can our team be from different campuses? ▼
Yes! Since this is an online hackathon, teams can include members from Main Campus, Jhelum Valley Campus, and Neelum Valley Campus. All coordination happens through the official digital channels.
What happens if we submit late? ▼
Late submissions are not accepted under any circumstances — no extensions, no exceptions. Submit as early as possible within the 8-hour window. Early submission gives you time to fix broken Drive links or missing folders before the deadline.
What do the top-performing teams win? ▼
Top-performing teams earn the opportunity to compete in the upcoming ONSITE hackathon — a real in-person event. All participants receive a certificate of participation. Top teams also benefit from mentoring, expert guidance, and the chance to build real portfolio projects.
How is the overall winner determined? ▼
Each of the 5 challenges is scored independently based on its own judging criteria. The overall winner is determined by the highest total score accumulated across all five challenges. Judges' decisions are final and cannot be appealed.
